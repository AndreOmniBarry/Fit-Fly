    Backup & restore

    Everything Fit Fly has ever saved on this device — every mini-app's history, your profile, and your preferences — as one file you keep yourself. Nothing here ever touches a server: exporting only ever saves a file to this device, and importing only ever reads one you choose. The Cycle Tracker's entries stay encrypted inside the file exactly as they are on this device — your PIN still unlocks them after a restore, and this screen never sees it.

    Importing replaces everything currently saved on this device with this file's data — this can't be undone.
